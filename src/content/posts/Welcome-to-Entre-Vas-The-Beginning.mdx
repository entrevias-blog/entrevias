# **Hi, hello!!**

Welcome to Entre Vias and welcome to my first post. Let get into it.

What is Entre Vías? Let’s explore it through the five Ws and an H: Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How.

I made this as a form of self expression for myself and hopefully you're able to connect with some of the things I share. In the age of AI, where everything is so carefully curated from emails to posts I want this to not be. So if you see some errors and some mistakes or I go on in a tangent let's appreciate that (but I will try my best to have it be good, I'm a capricorn sorry). 

In this space I would love to post more about travel and my itineraries and maybe create some for you? I would love to eventually get to a place where I can travel with others that I met through here but for now I guess we settle for my pictures and posts. 

I'll also share a bit about my engineering career and my journey toward figuring out my professional passions. Other things you might find here include my photography, training for a half marathon, my content creation journey, and honestly, anything else that comes up? 

If you’d like to follow along elsewhere, you can find me on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube at @xairaesp
